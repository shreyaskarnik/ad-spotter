City approves a 40-mile network of protected bike lanes

By Maya Chen · Updated 9:41 AM · 6 min read

The city council voted 7 to 2 on Tuesday to approve the new bike lane network, which will add 40 miles of protected lanes over the next three years. Opponents argued the plan would worsen traffic on Main Street and hurt small businesses that rely on curbside parking.

Supporters pointed to data from similar programs showing fewer injuries and more foot traffic for shops along protected corridors. Construction on the first segment is expected to begin in the spring.

Transportation officials said the first phase will focus on the corridors with the highest crash rates, including the stretch of Main Street between the river and the rail station, where two cyclists were killed last year.
